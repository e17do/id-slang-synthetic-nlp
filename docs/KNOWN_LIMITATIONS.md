# Known Limitations

## 1. Synthetic Nature

The dataset contains synthetic conversational examples.

It should not be treated as a direct record of naturally occurring conversations or as a statistically representative sample of Indonesian speakers.

## 2. Regional Metadata

`lokasi_dominan` represents the assigned context of a record.

It does not prove that an expression originated in, is exclusive to, or is universally characteristic of the stated location.

## 3. Coverage

The dataset is a finite release and does not represent all Indonesian slang, informal expressions, dialects, communities, generations, or historical periods.

Absence of an expression from the dataset does not imply that the expression does not exist.

## 4. Annotation Limitations

Sentiment and emotion labels are categorical annotations.

A word or expression can have different sentiment or emotional interpretations depending on context, speaker intent, and surrounding text.

## 5. Linguistic Ambiguity

Indonesian informal language frequently contains:

- spelling variation;
- abbreviations;
- code-switching;
- regional vocabulary;
- context-dependent meanings;
- rapidly changing internet expressions.

Automated labels cannot fully capture all such variation.

## 6. Historical Claims

The dataset does not establish the first appearance, historical origin, or complete evolution of an expression.

Temporal claims require additional evidence.

## 7. Geographic and Demographic Claims

The dataset must not be used to infer a person's:

- identity;
- geographic origin;
- ethnicity;
- demographic characteristics;

from a slang expression or assigned metadata.

## 8. Benchmark Limitations

Model performance measured on this dataset may not translate directly to naturally occurring Indonesian text.

Synthetic-data benchmarks should therefore be supplemented with appropriate real-world evaluation datasets where legally and ethically available.

## 9. Commercial Due Diligence

Potential commercial users should review:

- the repository license;
- release version;
- provenance documentation;
- schema and validation rules;
- known limitations;
- any applicable third-party rights.

## 10. Versioning

Limitations may change as the dataset evolves.

Users should cite and retain the exact release version used in experiments or production systems.
